## Description
ClubPulse is the all-in-one engagement and rewards platform for grassroots cricket clubs. Earn Scores by walking, playing matches, volunteering, and supporting your club — then spend them in the marketplace on real rewards.

**Stay Active, Earn Rewards**
Track your daily steps right from your phone and earn Scores every day. Hit your streak targets for bonus rewards that keep you motivated throughout the season and beyond.

**Match Day Made Easy**
See live fixtures, scores, and standings for your club. Check in on match days and follow the action as it happens.

**Play & Compete**
Join fantasy leagues, make predictions, and compete with fellow members. The more you play, the more you earn.

**Your Scores Wallet**
All your earned Scores in one place. Track your flexi balance, reward balance, and lifetime earnings. Know exactly what you have and how to spend it.

**Marketplace Rewards**
Redeem your Scores for cricket gear, club merchandise, coaching sessions, match tickets, and partner offers. Every Score counts.

**Club Admin Tools**
Committee members get built-in tools to manage members, track engagement, and run club operations — all from the same app.

Built for clubs. Powered by members. Rewarded for everything you do.
